01 / THE IDEAREALITY / IMAGINATION

A SPACE BETWEEN TWO SELVES

We've all imagined
being someone else.

Perhaps it isn't about disappearing,
but getting closer to ourselves.

From fictional characters, games and fashion to edited photos and habits borrowed from others, we find different ways to construct another self.

Drawing on personal experiences and interviews, this project imagines identity as particles that come together and drift apart. They emerge from reality, yet point towards who we hope to become.
